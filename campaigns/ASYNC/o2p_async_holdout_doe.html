1 - Executive Summary

  • Why: the 50/50 async test proved the mobile banner drives conversion (+0.198pp, +31%, consistent across cohorts). Keeping half the channel unexposed now costs conversions with no learning payoff.
  • What: serve the banner to 95% of the Action population as BAU; retain a randomized 4.75% no-banner holdout inside the channel.
  • Success: sustained lift in line with the experiment read, re-confirmed each cycle from the holdout contrast.
  • How long / how safe: standing BAU holdout, read per deployment cycle. At 4.75% the MDE (0.146pp) sits well below the proven effect, so measurement power is preserved (Section 4).

2 - Hypothesis & Objectives

  • Primary: H0: banner treatment effect = 0; H1: treatment effect > 0. Expected to re-confirm the completed experiment in steady-state BAU.
  • Secondary (optional): effect stability across deployment cycles; segment reads if cell volumes allow (mix-effect lesson from PCD Wave 4: report within-cell, never pooled-only).
  • Metrics: Primary = product 43 open (pre-approved overdraft), per reference_o2p_primary_secondary_definition; Secondary = 40/41/43 open; Guard rail = [ASK: unsubscribe is the template default but is email-specific. Propose banner dismissal/complaint rate, or none].

3 - Experiment Design & Treatment Assignment

  • Design type: two-proportion z-test (large n, binary conversion), same as the proven read.
  • Randomization unit: client (clnt_no).
  • Eligibility: O2P decisioned population, tactic IDs 2026099O2P / 2026126O2P / 2026132O2P family. Per tech spec §6.3 (o2p_offer_channel_trees.md): three segments PR (Premium) / NL (No Limit) / OT (Other eligible); the banner only serves report-group leaves 01/03/07, so the async contrast lives entirely inside those leaves. The holdout is carved INSIDE the channel: banner-eligible, randomized away from the MB banner only. It is not TG7.
  • Current design (per spec): TG4 = 95% Action, randomized 47.5% MB / 47.5% non-MB of total. This is a designed RCT within TG4. TG7 = 5% campaign control, untouched.
  • Proposed arms (the change): re-weight the TG4 internal split to 90.25% MB / 4.75% non-MB of total (= 95/5 of Action). TG7 stays 5%.
  • Split locked at assignment in a cell/TG registry BEFORE launch (PCD Wave 4 SRM was caused by an unregistered cell leaking in; z≈55 on one segment).

4 - Sample Size & Power Analysis

  • Baseline (from Async_tracker_Aug pivot, see o2p_async_tracker_aug_pivot_2026-09-04.md): NON_MB arm 0.628% tracker-fed average (6/18: 0.633%, 7/7: 0.623%); MB arm 0.817–0.835%; proven lift +0.198pp average (+31%). Cohort cadence ~3–5 weeks, ~470–543K Action clients/cycle.
  • MDE (two-proportion z, two-sided α=0.05, power 80%, 4.75/90.25 split of a ~505K cycle: holdout ≈ 24K, treated ≈ 481K, baseline 0.628%; live in o2p_holdout_mde_calculator.xlsx):
Read window Holdout n MDE vs proven lift +0.198pp
1 cohort (~505K) ~24K 0.146pp PASS (power 97%)
2 cohorts pooled (~1.0M) ~48K 0.104pp PASS, comfortable
(rejected) 2.5% holdout, 1 cohort ~12.6K 0.200pp FAIL: MDE ≈ the effect itself
  • Read: shrinking NON_MB from 47.5% to 4.75% widens the MDE 2.3× vs 50/50. Acceptable: the goal is monitoring a proven +0.198pp effect, not discovering a new one, and one cohort alone re-detects it at 97% power. 4.75% is the smallest share that still confirms within a single cycle; 2.5% fails that test.
  • What the change buys: ~216K clients per cohort move from no-banner to banner. At the proven lift that is ≈ +427 extra target responders per cycle.
  • Final sample size: one cohort suffices for the primary read; pool two consecutive cohorts for segment-level (PR/NL/OT) reads.

5 - Analysis Plan and Results

  • Quality checks: SRM (chi-square, 95/5 expected) every cycle; covariate balance (SMD) at first read.
  • Primary results: interim reads follow O'Brien-Fleming (pod convention; ~z 2.5 at interim, not 1.96); final per accumulated window. Results table per template: Leads | Treatment | Control | ATE | p | CI | $ Value (Size × lift × NIBT) [ASK: NIBT per conversion; verify units, a prior async slide shipped monthly NIBT labeled as 5-yr].
  • Subgroup: per-cell first, pooled second (mix-effect guard).
  • Multiple testing: single primary metric; no adjustment needed unless secondary 40/41/43 is promoted.

6 - Risks & Mitigation Plan

  • SRM from unregistered cells: lock the (strategy code, TG) registry pre-launch; SRM check gate on the first cycle.
  • Population definition drift (our mobile count ran +18.6% over a colleague's under the old IM filter): reconcile before launch so holdout denominators are agreed. Mobile = MB token only.
  • Holdout contamination (holdout clients seeing the banner via other surfaces): confirm suppression is enforced at deployment, not just at decisioning.
