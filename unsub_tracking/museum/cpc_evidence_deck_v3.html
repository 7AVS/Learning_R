Exploratory analysis · what we studied

The relationship between an email unsubscribe and the bank's consent record (CPC)

The question

When a client unsubscribes from a marketing email, what does their consent state in the Consent Preference Centre (CPC) look like, and does the unsubscribe relate to it?

An exploratory read. We describe what we monitored and what we observed.

The two data sources

Vendor email feedback (SFMC): every NBA marketing-campaign email unsubscribe, across roughly 30 active programs. Grain = email address.

CPC preference log: each client's consent switches and every change to them. Grain = client × switch.

The switches we monitor

1002Do-not-solicit (RBC Royal Bank). Blank counts as Yes.
1012Banking e-mail — email channel consent. Blank counts as Yes.
1014Share-for-Marketing across RBC — read by campaign decisioning to select clients. Blank counts as No.
Consent states: 5001 Yes · 5002 No · 5003 blank. On 1014 and 1015 an explicit No and a blank are one population and are counted together throughout this deck. On 1002 and 1012 a blank is a Yes, so No means an explicit No.

How we connect them

We follow the same clients. Of 319,733 who unsubscribed between July 2025 and June 2026, we join each on client number into their CPC records — every change within 90 days, and their standing position.

Email-to-client join: 1.12 addresses per client. The reverse direction, one address mapping to several clients, is not yet quantified.

Timing · when the CPC change was written relative to the unsubscribe

Of the 135 opt-outs recorded after an unsubscribe, 58 were written more than ninety days later

What we measured

Of the 319,733 unsubscribers, 1,387 hold an explicit opt-out on a monitored switch. 1,252 of those were already recorded before the client unsubscribed; 135 were recorded after.

The chart shows how long after the unsubscribe those 135 were written. 21 fall within the first week; 58 fall beyond ninety days.

The same test on blank writes returns two clients on each switch, every one of them written by the onboarding batch.

Explicit opt-out = a 5002 answer written on 1002, 1012 or 1014. Lag measured from the client's first unsubscribe.
