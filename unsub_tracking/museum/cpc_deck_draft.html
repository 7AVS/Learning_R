The system view

No single system in the chain treats no as no - and the systems do not talk to each other.

CoverageMTEC-12644 (blueprint, Jun 2026) addresses unsub -> consent-record write-back only. The delivery side of the chain is a confirmation question to system owners.
Confirmation questions: (1) which suppression list send-time reads; (2) which switch is the lawful campaign-email key, and its SLA; (3) does program-level express consent override a standing opt-out.
