## 1. L10b — THE AGE SKEW SURVIVES A CONTACT CONTROL. THE TENURE SKEW LARGELY DOES NOT.

This was the test that could have killed §1 of the exploration page. It did not.

**Age, ratio inside each contact band** (bank-wide ratio was 1.33 for `<25`, 1.27 for `25-34`):

| segment | 1 send | 2-3 | 4-6 | 7-12 | 13+ | bank-wide |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **age <25** | **1.63** | 1.35 | 1.27 | 1.25 | 1.31 | 1.33 |
| **age 25-34** | **1.76** | 1.34 | 1.21 | 1.18 | 1.21 | 1.27 |
| age 35-44 | 1.08 | 0.96 | 0.90 | 0.90 | 0.98 | 0.95 |
| age 45-54 | 0.75 | 0.80 | 0.77 | 0.80 | 0.78 | 0.78 |
| age 55-64 | 0.84 | 0.86 | 0.87 | 0.87 | 0.87 | 0.87 |
| age 65+ | 0.63 | 0.85 | 1.05 | 1.05 | 0.87 | 0.88 |

**VERDICT: contact volume is NOT the explanation for age.** Under-25s stay between 1.25 and 1.63 in
every band and never approach 1.00. The bank-wide 1.33 is a real age effect, not a mailing artefact.
The over-45 bands are equally stable on the protective side (0.75–0.87 throughout).

**Tenure, ratio inside each contact band** (bank-wide 1.25 for `0-2`, 0.82 for `20+`):

| segment | 1 send | 2-3 | 4-6 | 7-12 | 13+ | bank-wide |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **tenure 0-2** | **1.54** | 1.26 | **1.04** | 1.12 | 1.44 | 1.25 |
| tenure 3-5 | 1.36 | 1.15 | 1.04 | 1.05 | 1.26 | 1.15 |
| tenure 6-10 | 1.16 | 1.01 | 0.99 | 1.02 | 1.10 | 1.04 |
| tenure 11-20 | 0.86 | 0.86 | 0.97 | 0.96 | 0.95 | 0.92 |
| **tenure 20+** | **0.62** | 0.81 | **0.96** | 0.93 | 0.77 | 0.82 |

**VERDICT: tenure is PARTLY compositional.** At 4-6 sends, `0-2` collapses to 1.04 and `20+` rises to
0.96 — both essentially 1.00. The tenure signal is strongest at the extremes of contact (1 send and
13+) and nearly vanishes in the middle. New clients get mailed differently from long-tenured ones,
and some of the bank-wide tenure gap is that difference rather than a tenure effect.

**Consequence for the page:** say *"the young leave more"*, and treat *"the new leave more"* as
partly a targeting artefact. They are not equally solid findings and must stop being stated as one.

**VERDICT: no meaningful co-occurrence effect for cards.** The maximum lift across every cards pair

Brief 3c asked whether unsubscribes spike when specific campaigns run together. On this data the
answer is **no: campaigns cause unsubscribes roughly additively, and the combination adds at most
~3% over the worse of the two once the sample is large enough to trust.**

### OPEN DEFECT 1 — impossible solo rates leak into the table

`ACK` carries `rate_a = 735.99 per 1,000` (73.6%). `PIE` shows 659.86 in the bank-wide table, and
two pairs (`COB|PIE`, `PIE|VRE`) report `unsub_per_1000_est = 1000.00` on 33 and 36 clients where
`clients_est == leavers_raw` exactly.

Cause: `clients_est` sums weights — 1 for a leaver, 10 for a sampled non-leaver. When a campaign is
small enough that the 1-in-10 sample returns no stayers, `clients_est` collapses to the leaver count
and the rate goes to 1,000 per 1,000. `MIN_PAIR_LEAVERS = 30` does not catch it because the filter
is on **leavers**, not on estimated clients.

**Fix:** add a floor on `clients_est` (suggest ≥ 500) and a guard that suppresses any row where
`leavers_raw / clients_est > 0.5`. Nothing above 1.43 in the cards table survives that filter, so it
does not change the verdict — but these rows must not reach a slide.

### OPEN DEFECT 2 — `mne` values `1` and blank are being treated as campaigns

`mne_a = "1"` appears four times in the bank-wide top 25 and three times in the cards table; a blank
`mne_a` appears twice, on 151,053 and 32,775 clients. The bare-digit `1` was already flagged as
unexplained in `RUN_2026-07-29_full.md` §4. The blank is the `DEFAULT` stream arriving unmapped —
`senders_wide_raw` did not pass through `add_mne_program` in the version that produced this run
(fixed in commit 8840c03, after this run).

**Neither row can be quoted until the mnemonic is identified.** Rerunning L13 on the fixed code will
fold the blanks into `DEFAULT`; the `1` needs a look at `SUBSTR(TREATMENT_ID, 8, 3)` for that group.
