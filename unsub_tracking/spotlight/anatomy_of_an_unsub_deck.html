Anatomy of an Unsub · Cards / NBA
Unsubscribe spotlight · who we lose, and what changes after

We lose the channel to our best card spenders — but spending itself does not change when they unsubscribe

What we found

Who leaves: tiered leavers skew high-value — 41% High tier, and they averaged ~$2,450/mo card spend vs ~$2,050 for stayers. Leavers also peak at 4–7 years tenure and skew younger (<35).

What happens after — nothing: matched on starting tier, leavers hold their tier at the same rate as stayers (77.6% vs 76.1% High; 54.9% vs 54.5% Mid). The aggregate gap is who unsubscribes, not what happens after.

The cost is the channel, not the spend: high-spend clients go deaf to us. Three decisions follow — first-contact discipline on high-value lists · suppression fix as a standalone compliance ticket · a pre-launch unsub-cost guardrail for 1M+ blasts.

Profile numbers cover the 90.8% of unsubscribers matched to client attributes; the 9.2% unmatched are excluded. Tier mix covers tiered clients only — ~53% of leavers (vs ~29% of stayers) had no starting tier, concentrated in newly-acquired clients.
